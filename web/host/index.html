NoGoon · For hosts

List your event where people are looking for something real to do

NoGoon is a calm app that helps people step away from doomscrolling. Its Step Out tab lists farmers markets, cleanups, classes and community events near them. Listing yours is free.

How sign-ups work. NoGoon does not take sign-ups or collect attendees' details. If you add a sign-up link, the app sends people to your own form, and it tells them that opening the link does not sign them up until they finish your form.

What happens next

  • We read every listing before it appears. This usually takes a few days, and not every listing is accepted.
  • We may contact you at the email you gave to check details.
  • People can report a listing that is wrong or unsafe, and a listing reported several times is paused until we check it.
  • To change or remove a listing, reply to the email we send you or write to the contact address in the app.
