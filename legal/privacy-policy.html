Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 22, 2026

NoGoon ("we," "our," or "the app") is a habit-building Android application that helps users reduce unwanted screen time. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and a display name through Supabase Authentication.

Usage data. We store records of your in-app activity, including mini-game sessions (game type, score, points earned, timestamp), your blocklist of apps and websites (including which apps you chose to block, so it can sync across your devices), your Modes and settings, journal entries you choose to write, and your walk-away streak count. This data is stored in our Supabase database and linked to your account.

Accessibility Service data. NoGoon uses Android's Accessibility Service to detect when you open a blocked app or visit a blocked website. This detection happens entirely on your device. We do not transmit a history of the apps you open or the websites you visit to our servers. What is stored is your own blocklist and whether you successfully walked away (which updates your streak).

Step and motion data. The optional "Take your pal for a walk" feature, and the optional "Walk to get in" setting, count your steps using your phone's step counter or motion sensor (Android's Physical Activity permission). Step counts and walk totals are stored only on your device. They are not sent to our servers, and we do not use location for walks.

Purchase information. Subscriptions and point purchases are handled by RevenueCat and the Google Play Store. We do not store your payment card details. RevenueCat provides us with your subscription status (active or inactive) linked to your anonymous user ID.

Anonymous crash reports. If the app crashes, it can send a short report to our servers: the error message and technical trace, the app version, your Android version, your phone model, and the name of the screen. It does not include your name, email, account, device identifier, journal, blocklist or anything you typed. Email addresses and web-address query strings are removed before storage, and reports are deleted after 90 days. You can turn this off at any time in Profile > Setup & fixes > Send anonymous crash reports.

Briefing and event pictures. Some stories in the Briefing show a preview picture. Your phone loads each picture directly from the publisher's or Wikimedia's server, so that server can see your phone's IP address and the request, as with any web page. We do not send those servers your account or any other data. Tapping a story opens the publisher's own site, which has its own privacy policy.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the core app experience: tracking streaks, running mini-games, and maintaining your blocklist.
  • To sync your blocklist, Modes, and settings across your devices.
  • To manage your subscription status and point balance.
  • To improve the app based on aggregate, anonymized usage patterns.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS) with row-level security policies that ensure you can only access your own data. Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device using encrypted storage. All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the following service providers, solely to operate the app:

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase management
  • Google Play — app distribution and billing

5. Accessibility Service Disclosure

NoGoon uses Android's Accessibility Service exclusively to detect when you open apps or websites on your personal blocklist. This is the core functionality that lets the app intervene with a mini-game before you continue scrolling. The Accessibility Service does not collect passwords, read message content, or monitor any activity outside of the specific apps and domains you have blocked. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device's Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

NoGoon is for adults aged 18 and over, and asks you to confirm your age when you set it up. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data from inside the app (Me → Delete account), which removes your data immediately, or by emailing us at the address below. Upon receiving an emailed a deletion request, we will remove your data within 30 days.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or by email. The "Effective Date" at the top reflects the most recent revision.
